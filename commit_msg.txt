Render docstring numbered steps as a proper list in PDF report

Description sections previously joined every line of a test's docstring
into one dense run-on paragraph, making numbered step lists (the common
style across tests/test_regression_*.py) hard to read.

Add _docstring_flowables(): splits a docstring into blank-line-separated
blocks, merges wrapped continuation lines back onto their numbered step,
and renders any block containing numbered steps ("1. ...", "2. ...") as
separate hanging-indent paragraphs (one per step) instead of a single
Paragraph. Non-list blocks (intro/trailing prose) still render as plain
paragraphs, same as before.

Verified live against test_a4_loading_old_al4_config_files (steps
10-13) - the PDF now shows each step on its own line instead of one
run-on block.
